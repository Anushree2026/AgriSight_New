About Our Project

SafeVision: AI-based Real-time Weapon Detection System uses deep learning and computer vision to automatically detect weapons such as guns and knives from live CCTV feeds, providing instant alerts to enhance public safety.

Our Vision

Our Vision

We envision a safer future where intelligent surveillance systems detect weapons instantly and help prevent violent incidents before they escalate.

The Problem

The Problem

Manual CCTV monitoring is slow, error-prone, and often fails to identify weapons in real time. Increasing security threats demand automated AI-powered surveillance capable of detecting arms instantly and accurately.

Our Solution

Our Solution

SafeVision uses YOLOv8-based real-time detection to automatically identify weapons and notify authorities immediately:

▶ Instant visual & audio alerts
▶ Email notifications with detection snapshots
▶ Real-time dashboard monitoring
▶ Automatic logging of detection events

How It Works

How It Works

▶ Live CCTV feed processing through Flask
▶ YOLOv8 model detects guns, knives & suspicious objects
▶ Bounding boxes & confidence scores shown on dashboard
▶ Alert module activates instantly upon detection
▶ Detection data logged for future analysis
